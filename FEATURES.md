# Core Features of the Accounting Software

## 1. Account Management
- Create, edit, and delete accounts.
- Categorize accounts (e.g., assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses).
- View a chart of accounts.

## 2. Transaction Tracking
- Record income and expenses.
- Transfer funds between accounts.
- Split transactions into multiple categories.
- Attach receipts or other documents to transactions.

## 3. Reporting
- Generate financial statements (e.g., balance sheet, income statement, cash flow statement).
- Create custom reports with filtering and sorting options.
- Export reports to PDF or CSV format.

## 4. Budgeting
- Create budgets for different categories.
- Track actual spending against the budget.
- Receive alerts when approaching or exceeding budget limits.

## 5. Invoicing
- Create and send professional invoices.
- Track invoice status (e.g., sent, paid, overdue).
- Accept online payments.

## 6. User Management
- Invite and manage multiple users.
- Assign roles and permissions to users.
- Maintain an audit trail of user activity.
